English 10 Honors — Unit 3 Summative Task

Argumentative Synthesis Essay · Mr. D. Halvorsen · Period 2 & 5

Task

Drawing upon the texts in the Unit 3 packet as well as independent research, compose a well-developed argumentative essay (approx. 4–6 pp., MLA 9, double-spaced; Works Cited excluded from page count) that takes and defends a position on the following question: To what extent should schools restrict students’ access to personal devices during the instructional day?

Your essay must synthesize no fewer than three (3) sources, at least one (1) of which must be drawn from the packet and at least one (1) of which must be a source you locate independently that was published within the last five years. A dedicated paragraph must acknowledge and rebut at least one substantive counterclaim. Embed and cite evidence properly; block quotations, if used, do not count toward length. Note that “restrict” is intentionally broad — your thesis must define the scope of the restriction you are arguing for.

Process & deadlines

Annotated bibliography (3+ sources) submitted to Classroom by Fri 11/20, 11:59 pm. Rough draft uploaded to Turnitin by Mon 11/30, 11:59 pm (bring a printed copy to class on 12/2 for peer review — no printed copy = no peer review credit). Final draft due Fri 12/4 at the start of class via Turnitin; late submissions are subject to the departmental late policy.
