AP Environmental Science — Course Syllabus

Linden Hills High School · Fall Semester 2026 · Room 214 · Ms. R. Okafor (r.okafor@lindenhills.example)

Course description

This course provides students with the scientific principles, concepts, and methodologies required to understand the interrelationships of the natural world, to identify and analyze environmental problems both natural and human-made, to evaluate the relative risks associated with these problems, and to examine alternative solutions for resolving or preventing them. Students are expected to engage with laboratory and field investigations in accordance with College Board expectations, which constitute no less than 25% of instructional time.

Policies

Late work. Late assignments lose 10% per school day and are not accepted after three (3) school days. Lab reports are not accepted late without a documented absence.

Retakes. Students may retake one unit test per semester. Retakes must be completed within five school days of the test being returned, during office hours.

Absences. If you miss a test, you must schedule a make-up within two school days of returning or the test is recorded as a zero.

AI tools. You may use AI tools to study and review. You may not use AI to write any part of a lab report or the semester project. Suspected misuse is referred under the Academic Integrity Policy.

Phones. Phones go in the pocket chart at the start of class and stay there.
